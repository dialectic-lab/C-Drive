### About Me
*Designer, researcher, writer, artist.*  I'm a systems-thinker who spent the larger part of her career building inclusive, usable, and effective technology for education and finance. Currently researching and writing about financial inclusion as a design justice issue. My research process is artefact-led, driven by realities of technology impact on society. *[[content/Pages/About.md|More.]]*

- Advisory to non-profits and social impact startups.
