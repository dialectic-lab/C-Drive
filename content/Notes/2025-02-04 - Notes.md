Senthil Mullainathan
For the longest time, computing was seen as a **bicycle for the mind** - tools that let us expand our capacities. Something changed, and now we're at the framing that computing can do whatever the human mind can do. 
How did this happen?
Sociological concept: Common Task Framework (comparing progress in three areas - Computer vision| Large Language Models | Audio Processing)
- Here's a canonical task - like labelling images. The learning kept getting better and better at this. Competition drove progress.
- Progress was very slow in audio signal processing - no common dataset 
- Automation common tasks are easy. Augmentation tasks, not so much.
- This is instrumental in coming to the automation paradigm.
